A few things
to know.

Visa decisions are made by the relevant embassy, consulate or immigration authority.

Can you guarantee visa approval?

No. We assist with applications; we do not issue visas or decide outcomes.

What about fees and processing times?

Requirements, government fees and processing times can change. Ask our team for the current details for your destination. Processing times cannot be guaranteed.

Should I send documents with my enquiry?

Please don’t send passport scans or other identity documents through this website. Our team will explain how to share anything needed.
