What this website collects

This website does not have accounts, does not use advertising or analytics cookies, and does not store the details you type into its forms. The enquiry forms prepare a message inside your browser only.

When you contact us

If you choose to continue on WhatsApp or by email, the message you send is delivered through that service, and its own privacy policy applies. We receive whatever you choose to send, and use it to respond to your enquiry and provide the services you ask for.

  • Please don’t send passport numbers, passport scans or other identity documents through a website enquiry.
  • If a service needs documents, our team will explain how to provide them.

Links to other sites

Links to WhatsApp, Google Maps and other sites take you to services we don’t control.
